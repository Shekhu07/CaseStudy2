# Recruiting copy

Goal: **5–6 interviews**, plus as much survey volume as the channels give.
Recruiting takes 3–4 days, so this goes out before anything else.

Replace `<FORM LINK>` everywhere. Nothing here offers payment — partly because
there's no budget, mostly because paid respondents perform interest.

---

## 1 · Reddit — r/IndianFashionAddicts

The highest-yield channel by a distance. The Part 1 corpus found this sub without
naming it: 12 of 12 documents in an early probe came from here.

**Read the rules first, and message the mods before posting.** Several Indian
subs remove survey posts on sight or require prior approval. A removed post also
burns the account for a re-post, so ask first — it costs a day and saves the
channel.

> **Title:** Why do clothes sit in your Myntra wishlist and never get bought?
>
> I'm doing a product management case study on wishlists — specifically why
> things people genuinely want end up sitting there unbought for months.
>
> I've been through about 19,000 public reviews and comments, and the honest
> finding is that almost nobody writes about their wishlist. Plenty of people
> write about sizing being wrong or a return going badly, but the moment where
> you save something and then don't buy it is basically invisible. So I'm asking
> directly.
>
> It's 10 questions, about two minutes, anonymous: `<FORM LINK>`
>
> It asks you to actually open your wishlist while you answer, because nobody
> remembers this stuff accurately.
>
> If you're up for it there's an optional 30-minute call at the end where you
> walk me through your list item by item — that's where the interesting stuff
> comes out. Camera off is fine.
>
> Happy to post what I find back here once it's done. Not selling anything, not
> affiliated with Myntra.

## 3 · Reddit — r/india and similar general subs

**Probably skip.** Broad subs remove survey posts aggressively and the responses
are poorly targeted. The Part 1 gap-query run is the evidence: unanchored queries
into general subs returned 9.2% relevance against 26.1% for fashion-anchored
ones. Spend the effort on one good fashion-sub post instead.

## Interview scheduling — send to everyone who opts in

Send within 24 hours. Enthusiasm decays fast.

> Hi <name> — thanks for offering to do the wishlist walkthrough.
>
> It's about 30 minutes. You'd share your screen and we'd go through your saved
> items one at a time — I'm interested in what was going through your head when
> you saved each one, and what's stopped you since. There are no wrong answers
> and nothing to prepare.
>
> Camera off is completely fine. I'd like to record it so I don't have to take
> notes while you talk — let me know if you'd rather I didn't.
>
> Would any of these work? <give three specific slots>
>
> One ask: please don't tidy your wishlist beforehand. The mess is the point.

That last line matters. People clean up before showing you their list, and the
clutter is the finding.

## Practical notes

- **Post Reddit in the IST evening**, roughly 7–10pm, when the subs are busiest.
- **Screen for the stuck ones.** The wishlist-size and 30-day-purchase answers do
  this for you: prioritise interviews with people holding 15+ items who bought
  nothing. Someone with four items who buys them all has no problem to describe.
- **Don't add a "how did you hear about this" question.** Watch the response
  count before and after each post instead; channel attribution isn't worth an
  eleventh question.
- **Recruit 8 to land 5–6.** Some will not show.
